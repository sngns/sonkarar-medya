Riches Uploader

Riches Uploader is a private, single-user tool used by the owner of the Riches YouTube channel to upload and schedule his own edited Shorts to his own channel.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload) solely to upload videos the owner has reviewed and approved, and to set their title, description, tags and publish time. It is not offered to other users.
