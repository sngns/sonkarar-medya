Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Riches Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool operated by Berkay Şengüneş for uploading videos to his own YouTube channel, Riches.

Data the app accesses

The app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope. It uses this access only to upload videos and set their metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy and scheduled publish time) on the channel of the person who authorised it. It does not read, collect or analyse any other YouTube or Google account data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. No data is sent to or stored on any third-party server operated by the app. The app has no database, analytics or advertising.

Sharing

No user data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes stored access.
